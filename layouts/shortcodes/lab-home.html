The AI / Bio Fluid Engineering Laboratory investigates fluid-mechanical phenomena in biological systems and applies this knowledge to engineering and biomedical problems. We integrate computational fluid dynamics, experimental analysis, data-driven methods, and artificial intelligence to analyze and predict complex biofluid flows. Through quantitative flow analysis and modeling, we aim to advance the fundamental understanding of biofluid mechanics and develop accurate and efficient engineering and biomedical solutions.
